## The entry

> Dear Sir, I resign as your magician's assistant: after 300 shows sawn in half, I am taking my two halves somewhere with a lunch break.

134 characters — a magician's assistant, sawn in half 300 times, finally taking her two halves somewhere with a lunch break.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts   # writes out/entry.txt (and /output/entry.txt in the grader sandbox)
```

`main.ts` is deterministic: it writes the same fixed line every run, and self-checks the kit gate (1–140 chars, not whitespace-only, exactly one line) before writing. Node 22+ required (TypeScript runs natively).
